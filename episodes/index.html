HIT Technology Series
Hybrid Intelligent Transformer

Firmware-in-the-loop simulation of Hybrid Intelligent Transformer functions at data centre grid interconnections: series voltage regulation, harmonic mitigation, and ERCOT Model Quality Test response. HIT provides series voltage injection and local shunt functions; it does not perform the same role as shunt STATCOM or E-STATCOM equipment at the point of interconnection. Results shown are simulation outputs, not field-validated performance.
